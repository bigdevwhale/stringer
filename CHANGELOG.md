## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

The first issue.

- An editor's telegram before every walk: one ordinary thing to shoot, with a twist, on a telegraph form, with an «or» fallback and one swap.
- 1271 assignments in ten rubrics, in English and Russian, chosen by season, the weather you report and the evening light: three years of daily walks without a repeat.
- Frames shot with the phone's own camera, printed as a newspaper halftone, with the original one tap away; a one-line caption and the editor's mark in red pencil.
- The weekly issue as a newspaper page under your paper's masthead, shared as an image; the bound volume of every issue.
- A flash at dusk for the evening light.
- Investigations: the district's alphabet, digits 0–9, twelve doors, ten cats.
- The press card: ranks, a stamp per issue, frames by rubric.
- The district weekly or the big-city daily.
- No account, no internet, no camera permission.
